# VAYQUO — Company Scale Plan

## North Star
Build VAYQUO as a two-sided project marketplace, not as an affiliate landing page.

VAYQUO must increasingly own four layers:
1. Demand: the user starts and structures the project on VAYQUO.
2. Decision: VAYQUO determines what is relevant, compatible and comparable.
3. Supply: merchants and professional providers join VAYQUO directly or through verified partner integrations.
4. Economics: VAYQUO captures revenue from qualified contacts, transactions and later provider software.

## Revenue stack

### A. High-ticket project marketplace
Initial categories: kitchen, bathroom, photovoltaic, heating.
Revenue hypotheses to test:
- qualified-contact fee paid by providers;
- revenue-share through established lead partners during the bootstrap phase;
- later optional subscription/credits for providers.

No provider pricing is presented as fact until validated with signed partners and conversion data.

### B. Project commerce
Initial categories: workshop / garage, then Home Gym and living.
Revenue hypotheses:
- affiliate / merchant commission;
- later direct merchant revenue-share;
- sponsored alternatives only if clearly labelled and never allowed to override fit/compatibility logic.

### C. Provider software (later)
Only after VAYQUO has recurring provider demand:
- lead/project inbox;
- matching preferences;
- pipeline stages;
- quote follow-up;
- conversion analytics;
- subscription revenue.

## Defensibility
A generic AI recommendation box is not a moat. The moat must become:
- structured project data;
- compatibility and requirement rules;
- provider performance and close-rate data;
- merchant/provider supply relationships;
- price and outcome benchmarks;
- repeatable acquisition pages and shared project results.

## Stage gates

### Gate 0 — Product can create qualified intent
Required before scaling traffic:
- project category recognised;
- budget/region/context captured;
- result reached;
- primary revenue CTA measured.
Success metric: result completion and primary CTA rate are measurable by category and route.

### Gate 1 — Supply exists
Required before promising real matching:
- first provider registrations;
- partner categories and regions stored;
- direct outreach process defined;
- at least one active monetisation path per priority category.

### Gate 2 — First verified revenue
Required before paid acquisition:
- first externally verifiable lead/transaction revenue;
- revenue linked back to source/category where partner reporting allows;
- cancellation/invalid lead/return effects understood.

### Gate 3 — Repeatable unit economics
Scale only categories where contribution economics are positive.
Track:
- qualified intents per 1,000 sessions;
- provider/merchant CTA rate;
- valid lead / transaction rate;
- verified revenue per qualified intent;
- acquisition cost per qualified intent;
- contribution margin after refunds/invalid leads/traffic cost.

### Gate 4 — Marketplace expansion
Only expand categories when the current wedge has repeatable economics and supply coverage.

## Current priority
1. High-ticket home projects: kitchen / bathroom / PV / heating.
2. Workshop / garage commerce.
3. Home Gym / living as visual acquisition and secondary commerce until they outperform.

## Kill rules
- No new category without a supply/monetisation path.
- No paid traffic without measured conversion and revenue attribution.
- No fake live prices, fake savings, fake provider counts or fabricated testimonials.
- No feature work that cannot name the metric it is intended to improve.
- No dependency on a single affiliate/lead partner as the long-term model.

## Immediate operating loop
Every week:
1. Review revenue funnel by category and route.
2. Review new provider interests by category/region.
3. Identify the largest funnel drop-off.
4. Ship one change targeted at that metric.
5. Contact / onboard supply for the highest-value demand category.
6. Record verified revenue and invalid/returned outcomes.

This document is an execution framework, not a promise of financial results.
